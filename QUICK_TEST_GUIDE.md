Check browser permission:\n   - Reload page\n   - Allow microphone access popup\n   - Refresh and try again\n\n2. Verify microphone works:\n   - System Preferences → Sound → Input\n   - Select correct microphone\n   - Test in another app (Zoom, Skype)\n\n3. Check for errors in console:\n   - F12 → Console tab\n   - Look for red error messages\n   - Copy error and share in bug report\n```\n\n### Problem: Choppy/Delayed Audio\n**Solutions:**\n```\n1. Reduce number of participants:\n   - Works best with 2-3 people\n   - Each extra person = more CPU\n\n2. Close other apps:\n   - Chrome uses lots of CPU for WebRTC\n   - Close video, games, heavy apps\n\n3. Check network:\n   - Test speed: speedtest.net\n   - Minimum 1Mbps upload/download\n   - Move closer to router\n\n4. Try different browser:\n   - Chrome usually most stable\n   - Safari as backup\n```\n\n### Problem: One-Way Audio\n**Solutions:**\n```\n1. Both people joined audio room? (not just voice session started)\n2. Both allowed microphone permission?\n3. Check DevTools console for errors\n4. One person try: click \"Leave Audio\" then \"Join Audio\"\n```\n\n---\n\n## 📊 Testing Checklist\n\nUse this checklist to verify all features:\n\n```\n✅ Multi-Participant Audio\n  □ 2 people can hear (Simple Explanation)\n```\nWhen 3 people join audio room:\n\n┌─────────┐\n│  Alice  │────WebRTC────►┌─────────┐\n└─────────┘◄────WebRTC────┤  Bob    │\n    ▲                       └─────────┘\n    │                           ▲\n    │                           │\n    └───────WebRTC──────────────┘\n        (direct audio flow)\n        \n        ┌─────────┐\n        │ Charlie │────WebRTC────►Alice\n        └─────────┘◄────WebRTC────Charlie\n          ↓WebRTC↑\n\n✨ Each person connects directly to every other person\n✨ Server just coordinates, doesn't process audio\n✨ Very low latency, high quality\n```\n\n### Why Multiple Connections?\n```\n2 people:  1 connection (A↔B)\n3 people:  3 connections (A↔B, A↔C, B↔C)\n4 people:  6 connections (A↔B, A↔C, A↔D, B↔C, B↔D, C↔D)\n5 people:  10 connections...\n\nThis is why 10+ people get slow - too many peer connections!\n```\n\n---\n\n## 🚀 Next Steps\n\n1. **Test with real users** - 2-3 people in same circle\n2. **Monitor console logs** -
